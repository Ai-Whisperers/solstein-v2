> ⚠️ **DRAFT ONLY — NOT READY TO SEND.** These are unsent templates. Before any email goes out to a real recipient:
> 1. The **Michiel identity** problem must be resolved (see `gaps-before-send.md` G1).
> 2. The **legal posture** minimum must be in place (see `legal-posture.md`).

**Recipient:** Eneve CTO / CEO, or Vortex partner (Jorre Bonjer or Dalip Dewan most likely)
**From:** Michiel
**Subject:** Quick intro — AI-Whisperers transformation work

---

Hi `{FIRST_NAME}`,

Wanted to put you in touch with the team at AI-Whisperers. They've built a focused methodology and tool set for AI-native transformation of software-PE portfolio companies — exactly the kind of work that's been on your mind for `{ENEVE / VORTEX}`.

- They do fixed-fee diagnostics (€25K, 2 weeks) as a way to scope without committing

They've put together an outside-in read of `{ENEVE / YOUR PORTFOLIO}` that I think you'd find at least interesting. I'll let them share it directly.

If there's any appetite for a 30-minute exploratory call, they're happy to walk through what they see and — importantly — what they don't see, because they haven't been on-site.

`{SIGNATURE}`

---

### Notes for using W1

- **Do not send verbatim.** Michiel has their own voice. Give them the template as a reference; let them rewrite.
- The "Eneve or Vortex" branching depends on which direction the intro is going — `gaps-before-send.md` G1 + your understanding of the relationship context determines this.
- The "AI-Whisperers" firm name appears; this is fine once the legal entity is sorted.
- Do not mention specific pricing tiers — leaves room for us to calibrate once in the conversation.
- Do not over-claim credentials. This template deliberately does not say "they've transformed X companies" because we haven't.

### Notes for using W2

`{NAMED_PARTNER}`

### Notes for using W3

- **Gracious, not sycophantic.** Don't apologize or diminish. Just acknowledge and leave the door open.
- **The benchmark report offer** is useful if it actually exists. If we haven't produced our first benchmark report yet, don't offer it — promise something we deliver instead.
- **No auto-follow-up.** A decline is a decline.

## Template W4 — Post-first-call follow-up

**Context:** First exploratory call is done. They're interested or neutral.

**Recipient:** call attendee
**Subject:** Following up on our call — next-step options

---

Hi `{FIRST_NAME}`,

Thanks for the time. To recap what I heard and what we discussed:

**What we heard:**
- `{PRIORITY_1}` is the most pressing concern
- `{PRIORITY_2}` is secondary but significant
- `{CONCERN}` is a constraint we'd need to work around

**What we proposed:**
- A Phase 1 diagnostic scoped to `{SCOPE_AREA}`, 2 weeks, €`{PRICE}` fixed fee
- Deliverable: the transformation plan we walked through, plus a baseline metrics snapshot
- Access requirements: `{LIST}`

If this matches what you heard, next steps from our side:
1. We'll send a draft SOW by `{DATE}`
2. You review with whoever needs to review on your side
3. Countersignature, kickoff date confirmed, engagement starts within 14 business days

If anything doesn't match, let me know what to adjust. Happy to do a second call if easier than email.

Best,
`{NAMED_PARTNER}`

---

### Notes for using W4

- **"What we heard" recap** demonstrates listening and gives them a chance to correct. Do this in every post-call follow-up.
- **Specific next-step dates.** Don't say "soon" or "shortly" — name the day.
- **No upsell.** This is not the place to talk about Phase 2 or PPA. Close Phase 1 first.

## Template guidelines

- **Each template is a starting point.** Never send verbatim. Edit for voice, context, and the specific recipient.
- **One artifact per email.** If we have two things to share, we send one email and then a follow-up the next day. Don't dump everything in one message.
- **Clear subject lines.** "Following up on X" is always better than "Exciting opportunity!!!"
- **Signatures include a specific calendar / Calendly link.** Friction kills warm-intro momentum.
- **Never start an email with an apology** ("sorry to bother you..."). Start with the point.
- **Paragraph length.** 2-3 sentences. Partners at PE firms read on phones.

## Cadence rules

- After sending W2: wait 5 business days for a response. Do not follow up before that.
- If W2 gets no response after 5 days: send a single one-line follow-up. *"Hi X, just making sure this didn't get lost in your inbox. Let me know if I should re-send or if now isn't the right moment."*
- If single follow-up gets no response after 7 days: mark the lead as cold. Do not chase further. A cold PE lead is not a sale-able lead; pressuring them poisons the well.
