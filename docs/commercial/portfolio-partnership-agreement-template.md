## 1. Purpose

This Portfolio Partnership Agreement ("PPA") establishes the commercial framework under which `{PE_FIRM}` engages AI-Whisperers to deliver AI-native transformation services to companies within `{PE_FIRM}`'s portfolio.

The PPA does not commit `{PE_FIRM}` to specific engagement volumes. It does establish (a) pricing, (b) priority access, (c) information-sharing permissions, and (d) the standard SOW shape that governs each individual engagement.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Retainer-included services
The monthly retainer fee (§4.1) covers, for any company in `{PE_FIRM}`'s active portfolio:

- **Outside-in AI-readiness assessments** (non-diligence) — up to 4 per quarter. Turnaround 5 business days. Output: 3-5 page public-signal readiness brief.
- **Pre-investment diligence support** — upon request from `{PE_FIRM}`'s deal team during a live diligence process. Turnaround 48 hours. Output: a focused AI-readiness scorecard for the target, sized to fit a PE diligence workstream.
- **Quarterly portfolio benchmark report** — all active portfolio companies scored on our transformation-readiness rubric, delivered as a secure document to `{PE_FIRM}`'s operating partner group.
- **Methodology access** — `{PE_FIRM}`'s operating partners get read access to our internal methodology playbook and case study library under confidentiality.

### 2.2 Project-based services (priced separately)
The following services, when commissioned for specific portfolio companies, are governed by individual Statements of Work referencing this PPA:

- **Phase 1 diagnostic** (10-day engagement): priced at **€25,000 per engagement**, per the Phase 1 SOW template. Terms are the same as our standalone Phase 1 offering.
- **Phase 2 transformation pilots** (6-week scoped engagements): priced at **€40,000-60,000 per pilot** depending on scope, per individual SOW.
- **Phase 3 multi-quarter transformation engagements**: scoped individually, with equity-inclusive structures available per `§6`.

### 2.3 Service escalation
If the Provider's internal capacity becomes a constraint, Provider gives `{PE_FIRM}` 60 days' notice before any engagement declination. Provider agrees not to reduce `{PE_FIRM}`'s priority below existing clients without mutual agreement.

## 3. Priority access

Services to `{PE_FIRM}` portfolio companies take priority over cold-outbound engagements. Specifically:

- Phase 1 engagements for `{PE_FIRM}` portcos are slotted within 30 days of signed SOW; cold-outreach engagements slot within 60-90 days.
- Diligence-support requests are handled within 48 hours, taking precedence over non-diligence work.
- `{PE_FIRM}` operating partners have direct-line access to Provider's engagement leads (contact details shared separately).

Priority access does not translate to exclusivity: Provider may serve other clients, including other PE firms. If Provider takes on another PE firm within the same sector (e.g., another European mid-market software PE), Provider will notify `{PE_FIRM}` within 10 business days.

## 4. Fees

### 4.1 Monthly retainer
**€10,000 per month**, invoiced at the start of each calendar month, payable within 14 days.

The retainer is payable whether or not retainer-included services are used in a given month. This is intentional — the retainer covers Provider's reserved capacity, not per-use consumption.

### 4.2 Project fees
Individual engagement fees are invoiced per the governing SOW. Standard payment terms: 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery, 14-day payment net.

### 4.3 Fee reviews
Annually, fees may be adjusted by mutual agreement. Provider will not raise fees by more than CPI + 3% without `{PE_FIRM}`'s consent.

### 4.4 Late payment
Per EU Directive 2011/7/EU on combating late payment.

## 5. Information sharing

### 5.1 From `{PE_FIRM}` to Provider
To deliver services effectively, `{PE_FIRM}` agrees to share, under mutual confidentiality:
- List of active portfolio companies (updated within 14 days of any addition or exit)
- General diligence timelines and priorities (for planning purposes)
- Portco introductions as needed to enable Provider's engagements

`{PE_FIRM}` is not required to share financial details, deal terms, or internal investment committee materials.

### 5.2 From Provider to `{PE_FIRM}`
Provider shares:
- Quarterly portfolio benchmark reports (all companies, comparatively scored)
- Engagement summary reports at the close of each project (high-level outcomes; detailed reports go to the portco directly)
- Aggregate, anonymized industry trends observed across our client base (one report per year)

### 5.3 From Provider / `{PE_FIRM}` to portcos
Individual portcos receive their own engagement deliverables per their SOW. Neither `{PE_FIRM}` nor Provider shares one portco's diagnostic output with another portco without written consent from both.

## 6. Equity-inclusive engagements

For Phase 3 (multi-quarter) engagements, the individual SOW may include equity compensation in the portco (warrants, options, or direct equity) in addition to or partial replacement of cash fees. Equity arrangements:

- Are negotiated per-portco, not at the PPA level
- Require `{PE_FIRM}`'s cap-table review and consent
- Are capped at `{X}%` fully-diluted equity in any single portco
- Vest based on transformation milestones defined in the individual SOW

The default position is cash fees. Equity structures are considered only for engagements sized above €250K cash-equivalent.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will treat the other's non-public information as confidential. Obligations survive 3 years post-termination of the PPA or individual engagement, whichever is later.

Provider may produce anonymized case studies from engagements (no portco name, no identifying product or market details) for its own marketing purposes, unless a specific engagement's SOW explicitly prohibits this.

`{PE_FIRM}`'s name may be mentioned in Provider's marketing (e.g., "AI-Whisperers partners with `{PE_FIRM}` across its portfolio") with prior written consent on each instance.

## 8. Non-solicitation

For the duration of this PPA and 12 months following its termination, neither party will actively solicit employment of the other's engaged staff without prior written consent. Passive recruitment (public job ads, responsive LinkedIn conversations) is not prohibited.

## 9. IP and licensing

Provider retains ownership of the transformation methodology (frameworks, scoring rubrics, diagnostic templates, prompt library) under this PPA. Individual SOW-governed deliverables are licensed to the respective portco per the terms of each SOW.

`{PE_FIRM}`'s internal review and operating-partner use of Provider's methodology is permitted for the duration of the PPA. `{PE_FIRM}` may not redistribute the methodology outside its own organization or to portcos (portcos receive it via the engagement deliverables only).

## 10. Warranties and liability

Provider warrants that services will be performed with the skill and care reasonably expected of a professional engineering consultancy. Provider makes no warranties as to the business outcomes of the portcos.

Provider's aggregate liability under this PPA (excluding individual SOWs, which have their own liability provisions) is capped at the retainer fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

## 11. Termination

### 11.1 For convenience
Either party may terminate this PPA with 90 days' written notice. Any individual SOWs in progress at the time of termination continue to their natural completion under their own terms.

### 11.2 For material breach
Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice for material breach, provided the breaching party has had 15 business days to cure.

### 11.3 Transition
Upon termination:
- Monthly retainer ceases at end of the notice period
- Portfolio benchmark reports delivered up to that point remain `{PE_FIRM}`'s property
- Confidentiality obligations survive per §7

## 12. Governing law

## Appendix C — Economics summary (internal, not shared with PE firm)

Expected annual economics per PPA at steady state:

| Revenue stream | Low | Expected | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retainer (€10K × 12) | €120K | €120K | €120K |
| Phase 1 engagements (3-5/yr × €25K) | €75K | €100K | €125K |
| Phase 2 pilots (1-3/yr × €50K) | €50K | €100K | €150K |
| Phase 3 (0-1/yr, equity-inclusive) | €0 | €50K | €200K |
| **Total annual** | **€245K** | **€370K** | **€595K** |

Cost to serve: roughly 50-60% direct labor cost, so gross margin ~40-50%. A PPA covering one mid-market PE firm is a 1-1.5 FTE loading in steady state.

Break-even: the PPA pays for itself at ~3 Phase 1 engagements per year plus retainer. Anything above that is margin.

Risk: a PE firm exits its 2-3 largest portcos simultaneously → engagement pipeline drops sharply. Mitigate by not concentrating dependency on any single PPA; target 3-5 parallel PPAs at steady state.
