# Competitive positioning

Why a PE firm would choose AI-Whisperers over each of the obvious alternatives. Written for internal preparation — do not copy verbatim to client-facing material. Read this before any PE-firm conversation so objections land on prepared ground.

## The alternatives a PE firm actually has

When a PE firm decides to invest in an AI-native transformation, they choose from:

1. **McKinsey Digital / BCG Digital Ventures / Bain Vector** — the strategic consultancies' tech arms
2. **Accenture Song / Thoughtworks / EPAM** — the large system-integrator tech consultancies
3. **Boutique specialist firms** — smaller, sector-focused digital transformation consultancies (e.g., Gekko, 8th Light)
4. **In-house operating partner hire** — the PE firm hires a CTO-level operating partner
5. **Portco-level hire** — each portco hires a VP Engineering / CTO
6. **Do nothing** — decide it's not worth pursuing

We compete with all six. Honestly, not gracefully.

## Head-to-head

### vs. McKinsey Digital / BCG / Bain

**Their advantages:**
- Decades of relationships with PE firms; the partner walking in is usually someone the LP has worked with for 15 years
- Deep benches; if a lead engineer gets hit by a bus, there's another behind them
- Global delivery; they can match a client's geographic footprint
- Career-cover: the PE partner can say "we paid McKinsey €1M" and not lose the next fund-raise if it goes poorly
- Full-stack: they'll do strategy, change management, communications, and implementation under one roof
- Indemnification + €50M+ liability insurance

**Our advantages:**
- 2-5× faster delivery on the specific transformations we do
- No partner-tax in our pricing; the lead engineer is doing the work
- Methodology published and reviewable; clients can see what we do before buying
- Honest scoping — we don't expand scope during engagement
- No vendor lock-in pressure

**Our disadvantages:**
- Zero track record vs. their hundreds of engagements
- 2-person team vs. their thousands
- No global presence
- No career-cover for the PE partner

**Honest positioning:** we are not a McKinsey replacement. We are a narrower, faster, specialist option for firms that want targeted AI-native transformation without the MBB overhead. A sophisticated PE partner may choose us *alongside* MBB (we do the engineering; MBB does the communications/strategy/change management). That's the compete-and-complement story — not replace.

### vs. Thoughtworks / Accenture Song / EPAM

**Their advantages:**
- Bench depth (hundreds of engineers available)
- Sector-specific practices (Thoughtworks has retail, healthcare, etc.)
- Enterprise procurement muscle
- Established engineering reputation

**Our advantages:**
- AI-native by default (many of them still treat AI as a feature, not a platform)
- Faster mobilization (weeks vs. months)
- Smaller-engagement pricing (their minimums are often €500K+)
- Closer partner attention per engagement

**Our disadvantages:**
- Bench
- Reputation
- Client list

**Honest positioning:** for engagements below ~€500K / 6 months, their pricing and setup overhead price them out. We fit that band — but only within that band. Above it, we can't compete.

### vs. Boutique specialist firms

**Their advantages:**
- Sector expertise we don't have (if they're in fintech, utilities, etc.)
- Established PE relationships
- Track record

**Our advantages:**
- Pure AI-native focus (most boutiques are generalist digital transformation, not AI-specifically)
- The methodology playbook is a differentiator; most boutiques rely on partner tacit knowledge
- Smaller and more responsive

**Our disadvantages:**
- Their existing relationships; they're already in the room we're trying to enter
- Sector depth

**Honest positioning:** this is the closest competitor set. We differentiate on AI-native specificity, methodology transparency, and the "we've instrumented our own work" proof-point. In a head-to-head without warm-intro advantage, boutiques with PE relationships win; with warm-intro on our side, we're competitive.

### vs. In-house operating partner hire

**Their advantages:**
- Loyalty (fully inside the firm)
- Continuous attention
- Cap table and comp alignment
- Deep PE-network fit

**Our disadvantages:**
- Permanent not portfolio
- Not billed externally
- Dilutes the "external specialist" signal

**Our advantages:**
- Methodology we bring that a hire would have to develop
- Cost flexibility (pay per engagement vs. permanent salary)
- Multi-firm expertise (we see patterns across PE firms)
- Honesty — a hired operator may hesitate to recommend archive-and-rebuild on a portco the hiring partner championed

**Honest positioning:** we are complementary, not competitive. A good operating-partner hire plus us is stronger than either alone. The PE firm gets strategic continuity from the partner and specialized execution from us.

### vs. Portco-level hire

**Their advantages:**
- Permanent in the portco
- Integrated with the business
- Long-term relationship with the engineering team

**Our advantages:**
- Already done the pattern; a VP Eng learning AI-native is 12-18 months behind
- External perspective; internal leaders often can't see the legacy patterns they've normalized
- Full methodology available from day one

**Our disadvantages:**
- Temporary by design

**Honest positioning:** we accelerate the hire's first year. A VP Eng hired in month 0 who engages us in month 2 can deliver in month 6 what they'd otherwise deliver in month 18. We are not a replacement for the hire.

### vs. Do nothing

**Their advantages:**
- €0 spent
- No disruption
- Zero risk

**Our advantages:**
- Compounding opportunity cost of delay
- Competitors are also considering this; standing still = falling behind
- Valuation impact at exit

**Their real concern:** engagement risk vs. benefit. If we can't underwrite a specific ROI on a specific pilot, "do nothing" is the rational default.

**Honest positioning:** our Phase 1 diagnostic is specifically designed for the "not sure if this matters" buyer. €25K to find out, standalone value if you don't proceed. If we can't persuade them in Phase 1, they're correct to do nothing.

## The positioning table a PE partner will think through

| If they want... | They should choose... |
|---|---|
| Full-stack transformation with career-cover | MBB Digital |
| Enterprise-scale execution at €500K+ / 6+ months | Thoughtworks / EPAM / Accenture |
| Sector specialist who already knows utility billing (or healthcare, or fintech) | Boutique in that sector |
| A permanent operating partner across portfolio | In-house hire |
| A permanent technical leader at one portco | Portco-level CTO |
| AI-native specialist, narrow, fast, for a specific 2-8 week engagement | **Us** |

If a PE partner wants what's in the last row, we win. If they want any of the other rows, we lose — and we should be honest about which row this particular partner is in.

## Where we are on the credibility curve (today, April 2026)

Honestly:
- **Reputation:** emerging. Zero published case studies beyond our own internal one.
- **References:** zero external. This is the single largest credibility gap.
- **Team:** small and unnamed in commercial documents.
- **Track record:** one internal rebuild. Honest about this being a demonstration, not an enterprise engagement.
- **Methodology:** published, reviewable, defensible. Our strongest asset.
- **Commercial machinery:** templates exist but untested. Have not signed any external agreement.

The path to actual competitiveness: one or two low-priced (or pro-bono) paid engagements with public case studies produced from them. Without that, we cannot credibly displace any of the alternatives above.

## What we say when asked "why you?"

Three sentences. Do not say more:

1. *"We're narrower and faster than MBB or Thoughtworks for the specific kind of transformation you need. Our methodology is published and reviewable before you engage us — unusual in this market."*

2. *"We're transparent about what we haven't delivered yet. This would be one of our first external engagements. We're structuring pricing and terms to reflect that honestly — partial discount, reference rights, milestone-based payments."*

3. *"If what you want is one-stop-shop strategy + implementation for €1M+, we're not that. If what you want is specific AI-native transformation delivered in 2-8 weeks at €25-100K per engagement, the methodology is built for exactly that."*

## Anti-patterns (things not to say)

- *"We're the best at this"* — we have no basis for that claim.
- *"McKinsey is slow and expensive"* — true in some ways, insulting in context.
- *"We've done this for other PE firms"* — we haven't.
- *"Don't bother with the alternatives"* — false and reads as desperate.
- *"AI-Whisperers is cheaper"* — cheaper doesn't sell; cheaper costs often end badly.
