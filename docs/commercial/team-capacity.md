## The honest capacity picture

If the firm is currently 1-2 people actively available:

**We can credibly commit to:**
- 1 active Phase 1 engagement at a time
- 1 ongoing Phase 2 pilot at a time (not simultaneously with a Phase 1)
- 0 Phase 3 engagements without hiring

**We cannot credibly commit to:**
- 3 Phase 1 engagements per year per PPA (that's one per quarter, probably feasible)
- A retainer service level that includes 4 assessments/quarter + 48-hour diligence + quarterly benchmarks
- Multiple simultaneous PPAs

**The PPA retainer promise (€10K/month) assumes capacity we don't have.** Either:
- Reduce retainer scope (only 1 assessment/quarter + quarterly benchmark, no diligence-support SLA)
- Raise retainer price to reflect real capacity needed (€15-20K/month)
- Hire before signing any PPA
- Accept that first PPA signatory gets priority and we cap to 1 PPA at any given time

## Pre-external-motion checklist

Before any outreach starts:

- [ ] The team table above is filled in with names
- [ ] Each person in the table has signed a commitment (engagement contract, employment agreement, partner agreement) that binds their availability
- [ ] Capacity is reconciled against commercial promises (Phase 1 count, PPA count)
- [ ] Bench-depth risk is addressed: if the lead engineer is unavailable for 2 weeks, who steps in?
- [ ] Career-coverage risk is addressed: if the partner is unavailable, who leads the presentation?

These are founders' decisions, not documentation decisions.

## If the team is currently just one person

That's still enough for:
- 1-2 Phase 1 engagements per year, delivered rigorously
- Methodology development
- A single case study that proves delivery capability

It's not enough for:
- Any PPA
- Parallel engagements
- A "we have a team" pitch

Before pitching any PPA, the team must credibly have 2 engineers + 1 partner minimum, with pipeline for a 3rd.

## Hiring gates

At each hire, write down:
- Why we're hiring (specific engagement or sustained capacity?)
- What skills are filled by this hire that the current team doesn't cover
- Revenue required to sustain the hire for 12 months
- What work the hire does in their first 90 days

Without this discipline, hires happen in anticipation of revenue that doesn't arrive, and the firm runs out of cash.

## Partner-time protection

The partner is the choke-point. Every engagement consumes partner time on:
- Scoping / SOW drafting
- Kickoff meetings
- Mid-engagement check-ins
- Final presentations
- Post-engagement follow-up
- Sales calls for next engagements

At 2 Phase 1 + 1 Phase 2 per quarter, partner time is roughly 20-25 days/quarter on engagements alone — before sales, admin, methodology updates. If the partner is also the commercial lead, expect overload at this volume.
