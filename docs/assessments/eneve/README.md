## Assessment conclusion (from `outside-in-assessment.md`)

Eneve is a textbook Vortex-backed rollup mid-consolidation — 4 merged predecessor codebases, zero external AI signal, no public tech brand, 130+ employees, €30M revenue, 50+ enterprise energy clients. Every public indicator (careers, GitHub, marketing language, news cadence) places them at the beginning of an AI-native transformation, not the middle. The Vortex investment thesis explicitly names "accelerating software and digital technology companies," which means the PE sponsor is receptive.

**Recommendation:** proceed to prepare a Phase 1 diagnostic proposal. Engage primarily through the Vortex relationship.
