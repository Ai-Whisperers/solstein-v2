## Why this document exists

During preparation for a possible Eneve engagement, research surfaced the broader context: **Eneve is one of 25+ platform companies in the Vortex portfolio.** Vortex's investment thesis explicitly names *"accelerating software and digital technology companies"* and lists AI/ML as a focus sector. Vortex has 2 dedicated operating partners with tech backgrounds (Dalip Dewan, ex-CTO/CIO; Stefan Schinkel, 30y GTM tech), a technical analyst (Dragos Prodan, software engineering background), and has completed 75+ add-on acquisitions — meaning most portcos are carrying integration debt.

This suggests the commercial opportunity is **larger than a single-portco engagement**. A portfolio-wide transformation partnership with Vortex would:
- Provide recurring revenue (not single-engagement)
- Lower customer acquisition cost (one relationship, N engagements)
- Align with Vortex's stated thesis explicitly
- Leverage Vortex's operating-partner infrastructure as our go-to-market

This document assesses which portfolio companies fit the Eneve-like transformation profile, and how to structure the offering.

## Why this portfolio specifically

Three structural signals make Vortex's portfolio unusually well-suited for our offering:

### 1. High add-on density
75+ add-on acquisitions across 25 platforms = average ~3 add-ons per platform. Every add-on creates code-integration debt. Eneve's 4-way merger is the extreme example; Bloxs, Kitry, POM, Hallo all show the same pattern at smaller scale.

### 2. Explicit AI thesis + operating infrastructure
Vortex lists AI/ML as a focus sector. They have Dalip Dewan (ex-CTO/CIO operating partner) and Dragos Prodan (software engineering technical analyst) internally — meaning they understand tech-debt when it's shown to them. Unlike generalist PE firms, they don't need to be convinced that modernization matters.

### 3. Mid-market Benelux focus
The portfolio companies are sized for the kind of engagement we can deliver (€10M-€600M revenue, 30-3,000 employees). Smaller than MBB's sweet spot, larger than boutique-consultancy scale. Exactly the segment we're designed for.

## The proposed offering: Portfolio Partnership Agreement

### Structure

| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Multi-year framework agreement with Vortex |
| **Pricing** | Monthly retainer (€10-15K) for ongoing portfolio-wide benchmarking + per-portco engagement fees at the Phase 1 / Phase 2 rates we use for single engagements |
| **Scope** | Right of first review on every new platform investment (AI-readiness assessment as part of diligence); right to pitch Phase 1 to every portco; portfolio-level benchmark reporting quarterly |
| **Exclusivity** | Soft — no exclusive right for us, no exclusive obligation for them |
| **Term** | 2 years initial; rolling 1-year renewals |
| **IP** | We retain methodology; deliverables to individual portcos licensed per the standard Phase 1 SOW |

### What Vortex gets

- **Pre-investment AI-readiness assessment** on every target under diligence — 48-hour turnaround, outside-in, no access needed
- **Portfolio benchmark report quarterly** — where does each portco sit on our transformation scorecard vs. the portfolio median
- **Preferred access** to us for Phase 1 and Phase 2 engagements with any portco
- **A transformation methodology** Vortex can point to in their own marketing and fundraising ("Vortex partners with AI-Whisperers for portfolio AI transformation")
- **Operating-partner bench extension** — Dalip Dewan's 30-year CTO experience + our methodology + our execution capacity = an unfair advantage vs. generalist PE firms

### What we get

- **Pipeline certainty** — 2-4 Phase 1 engagements per year from Vortex portcos, plus ad-hoc diligence work
- **Commercial velocity** — no cold-outreach, no relationship-building from zero
- **Cohort pricing power** — as our portfolio benchmark grows, the data itself becomes a moat
- **Compounding reputation** — successful engagements with Vortex-backed companies become references for the next Vortex
- **One-commercial-contact** — manageable relationship management

### Pricing math (internal only)

Assume 3 Phase 1 engagements per year from Vortex (€25K each = €75K) + retainer (€10K × 12 = €120K) + 1 Phase 2 pilot per year (€50K). **Annual revenue from Vortex = ~€245K**, likely higher.

Comparable cold-outreach effort to land 3 Phase 1 engagements in a year: ~€40K in commercial time + founder time + travel. Vortex partnership removes most of this.

## Risks and failure modes

Flag these before proceeding:

### R1 — Vortex already has a preferred transformation partner
We don't know if Vortex has an existing relationship with a consultancy (McKinsey Digital, accenture, EY) or an in-house team that claims this ground. Our warm-intro contact should probe this before we pitch.

### R2 — Vortex's operating partners are defensive
Dalip Dewan and Stefan Schinkel may view us as encroaching on their remit. Frame our offering as *augmenting* their capacity, not replacing it. Position: "we're the methodology + execution arm; they're the strategic + relationship arm."

### R3 — Portfolio companies don't want to be prescribed
A portco can reject a Vortex-recommended partnership. Our Phase 1 offering has to stand on its own merits after the intro; the intro just opens the door.

### R4 — A single bad engagement contaminates the whole portfolio
If we bungle the first Vortex portco engagement, every other portco hears about it. The first engagement has to be among our best work — consider taking a smaller fee on it in exchange for reference rights.

### R5 — Vortex exits Eneve before we deliver
Vortex's typical hold period is 4-7 years. Eneve entered in 2022 → exit window 2027-2029. Plenty of time. But if they're preparing for exit, the appetite for new consulting engagements drops. Diligence the timeline.
